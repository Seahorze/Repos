Slow Cooker Potluck Spare Ribs

These crockpot spare ribs are perfect for a potluck. Use a slow cooker bag and cleanup is super easy. It's sure to be a recipe they beg for!

Ingredients

  • 6 pounds pork spare ribs, cut into serving-size pieces
  • 1 ½ cups ketchup
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • ½ cup vinegar
  • ½ cup honey
  • ⅓ cup soy sauce
  • 1 ½ teaspoons ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ¾ teaspoon ground mustard
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Step 1

    2. Gather all ingredients.

  2. Step 2

    3. Put spare ribs into the bottom of a slow cooker. Top with ketchup, brown sugar, vinegar, honey, soy sauce, ginger, salt, mustard, garlic powder, and black pepper.

  3. Step 3

    4. Cover and cook on High for 5 hours.
